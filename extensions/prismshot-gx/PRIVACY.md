# Privacy Policy

PrismShot GX performs its work locally inside the user's browser.

## Data processed locally

The extension may locally process:

- Pixels from the selected video frame or visible browser tab
- The current page title and hostname for filenames
- Video playback time and dimensions
- User-selected capture settings
- Small recent-capture thumbnails and metadata

## Data transmission

PrismShot GX does not transmit captured images, browsing history, page URLs, titles, settings, analytics, or identifiers to the developer or any third party.

## Storage

Settings are stored with the browser's extension storage. Recent-capture thumbnails and metadata are stored locally and can be cleared from the settings page. Full screenshots are written only to the browser's Downloads destination or clipboard as requested.

## Permissions

- **Read and change data on websites**: detects video elements and places the optional capture toolbar.
- **Active tab and scripting**: inspects and captures the selected active tab, including frames loaded before installation.
- **Downloads**: saves requested screenshots.
- **Clipboard write**: copies requested screenshots.
- **Offscreen document**: crops, resizes, encodes, and copies image data without opening a visible tab.
- **Web navigation**: discovers nested frames containing video.
- **Context menus**: adds video capture actions.
- **Storage**: saves settings and local recent-capture metadata.
