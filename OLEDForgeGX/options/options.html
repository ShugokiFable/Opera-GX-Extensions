Privacy & hard limits

OLED Forge processes page styles inside your browser. It has no analytics, no remote API, no account system, and no network service.

A browser extension cannot change monitor firmware, Windows HDR calibration, OLED compensation cycles, browser chrome, or the pixels inside protected DRM video. It can only alter normal webpage rendering.
